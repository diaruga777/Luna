# Luna Reader

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the current app identities, architecture,
workflow, device automation rules, and build/test instructions.

Luna Reader is the former Luna Core app (`com.hsanabria.luna`, Xcode scheme
`LunaReader`). The App Store listing remains **Luna: Save Anything**.
The former standalone Reader, Shopping/Price Tracker, and Downloader apps are
retired; do not recreate their targets or extensions. Legacy storage and file
identifiers remain for compatibility. Feature details live in `claudedocs/`.
