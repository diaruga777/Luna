The bundled clip is a recut, not the source file. The source renders one frame
per hour of 2026; this takes the run from 14 Dec 2026 04:00 UT (a 22%-lit waxing
crescent) to the full moon of 24 Dec 2026 02:00 UT, every second hour, scaled to
960x540 — then plays it backwards to its own first frame, so the loop has no
seam and the moon waxes and wanes forever. December's is the year's closest full
moon (apparent diameter 2009"), which is why that lunation and not another.

The clip is used for factual/educational demonstration. No NASA endorsement is
stated or implied.

Reproduce with:
  curl -O https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a005500/a005587/phases_2026_plain_720p30.mp4
  ffmpeg -i phases_2026_plain_720p30.mp4 \
    -vf "select='between(n\,8332\,8570)*not(mod(n-8332\,2))'" -vsync 0 frames/f%04d.png
  # concat frames forward, then reversed without its duplicated endpoints, at 30fps
  ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i list.txt -vf scale=960:540:flags=lanczos -r 30 \
    -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 26 -preset veryslow \
    -tune film -movflags +faststart -an LunaMoonPhases.mp4
